VERSION=1.9.11

[NEW FEATURES]
PocketMC is now available in Simplified Chinese. Switch languages in Settings > Language & Region, or add your own language pack under i18n.

Server downloads now fall back through multiple mirrors (GitHub accelerators and BMCLAPI) and retry automatically, so a blocked or slow network no longer stops an install. Add your own mirror in Settings > Downloads & Mirrors.

PocketMC now recognizes hybrid server software such as Mohist, Arclight, Magma, CatServer, Purpur, Spigot and CraftBukkit when importing or launching an existing server folder.

[IMPROVEMENTS]
Forge and Vanilla servers can now be created down to Minecraft 1.7.10.

The Runtimes section is now called Downloads.

[FIXES & STABILITY]
Downloads resume from where they stopped and verify hashes after every mirror hop.
